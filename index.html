Questions

Common questions

What happens during an online Ayurvedic consultation?

Your practitioner will assess your Prakriti (physical and mental constitution), current health concerns, lifestyle, diet, and stress levels. Using classical Ayurvedic diagnostic methods — pulse reading, tongue assessment, and detailed questioning — they'll identify the root imbalances and create a personalised plan.

How long is the consultation, and what do I receive?

The initial consultation is 45 minutes. Following the session, you'll receive a written care plan (PDF) within 24 hours covering your herbal protocol, dietary recommendations, daily routine, yoga or pranayama practice, and follow-up schedule.

Do I need any preparation before the session?

Complete the intake form we send after booking. Have any recent medical reports or test results handy. Find a quiet, well-lit space with a stable internet connection. Avoid a heavy meal for at least 2 hours before the session.

How is this different from a general health check-up?

Ayurveda treats the root cause of imbalance rather than the symptom in isolation. Your practitioner looks at your unique constitution, digestive fire (Agni), toxin accumulation (Ama), and the interplay between your physical, mental, and spiritual health — offering a whole-system understanding of your wellbeing.

Can Ayurveda work alongside my existing medical treatment?

Yes. Ayurveda is complementary to modern medicine. Always inform your practitioner of any medications or treatments you're currently undergoing. They will tailor your Ayurvedic protocol to safely support your existing care without interference.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Full refunds are issued for cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled session. Cancellations within 24 hours receive a credit note valid for rescheduling within 30 days. No-shows are non-refundable.
